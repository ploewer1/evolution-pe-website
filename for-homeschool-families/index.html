Made for home

Designed around how homeschool PE really works

Solo, partner, and group

Every game has a version for one child, one child with a helper, and groups of 4 to 20, so nobody waits for a teammate.

Everyday equipment

Each game lists what you need and household alternatives, such as paper plates, rolled socks, or tape on the floor.

A full 36-week year

Two tracks, K–2 and 3–5, move through movement skills, games, rhythm, fitness, health, and a celebration week.

Printable cards and sheets

Game cards, setup diagrams, and recording sheets are designed to print at home.

Pairs with Bench Boost

Weekly topics are designed to connect to Bench Boost, where children can practice what they learned.

Calm, skill-based play

When a round goes wrong, children rejoin through a skill, not by doing exercise as a penalty.

Safety first

Activities need adult supervision and should be adapted to each child. Talk with your child's doctor if you have questions about what is right for them. Stop any activity that causes pain or dizziness.

Good to know

Questions families ask

Can I buy the curriculum now?

Not yet. It is still being finalized, and nothing on this site takes payments. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know when it's ready.

Is the free sample lesson available?

Not published yet. Ask for it on the waitlist and we'll email it when it's ready.

What ages is it for?

Kindergarten through Grade 5, in two tracks: K–2 and 3–5.

Do I need special equipment?

Games list what you need and household alternatives. Many use things like soft balls, beanbags, cups, or tape.

Can I use it with just one child?

Yes. Every game has a solo version that does not need a teammate or an opponent.
