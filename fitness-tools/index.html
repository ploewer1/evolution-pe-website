Fitness Test Analyzer

Load a class's results from a spreadsheet, or type scores in, and see totals by grade and gender. Choose what to compare against.

Option 1

Your school's FitnessGram results

Uses the "Standard Met" columns that your school's FitnessGram software already calculated.

Option 2

Presidential test (2026)

Built-in benchmarks from the official White House table published in May 2026, for ages 6 to 17+.

Option 3

Your own standards

Enter your state or district standards by age and sex, and save them in a small file to reuse.

What it does

  • Upload an Excel or CSV file, or type or paste scores
  • Class totals by grade and gender, plus Pre to Post movement
  • Individual results on screen to see who met each standard
  • Excel summary and print report with no student identifiers

How it handles data

  • Designed to run in your browser, without uploading or saving student records
  • Nothing is stored: typed scores stay on the page until you close it
  • Built with testing in mind, but still in beta and not independently audited
