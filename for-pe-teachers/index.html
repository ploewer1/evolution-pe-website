Good to know

Questions teachers ask

Can I use the analyzer today?

Yes, it's open in beta. It runs in your browser and is designed not to upload or save student records, but check results against your official reports and follow your district's rules. Join the teacher list below to get updates.

Can I use it with FitnessGram data?

The analyzer can summarize results using the "Standard Met" columns that your school's FitnessGram software already calculated. Evolution PE is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by The Cooper Institute.

Is the curriculum ready to use?

Not yet. It is being finalized. Join the list and we'll tell you when it's ready.

Is it aligned to national or state standards?

We haven't published a standards alignment, and we don't claim endorsement by any standards organization. If an alignment is documented when the curriculum is released, we'll publish it.

Can my class use Bench Boost?

Bench Boost is open at learn.evolutionpe.com and is still in beta. Check your school's rules about student use of online tools first.
