Bench Boost

Interactive PE and health learning

A web app where students practice physical education and health ideas through short, game-style activities across four grade bands.

  • Grade bands K–2, 3–5, 6–8, and 9–12
  • Movement, fitness, teamwork, and health topics
  • Multiple practice modes, from Learn to Lightning Round

Actively in development. Content and features are still growing.

Who's behind it

Created by an elementary PE and health teacher

Evolution PE exists because teachers and families deserve physical education resources that are easy to run, include every child, and are honest about what they can and can't do.

  • Movement is never punishmentGames use skill-based re-entries, not exercise as a penalty.
  • Everyone can playSolo, partner, and group versions of every game.
  • Sources shownHealth connections name where the information comes from.
  • Honest about statusIf it isn't ready, we say so on the card.
