Works with the curriculum

Teach it in the gym, practice it in the app

The K–5 curriculum is designed so each week's topic connects to Bench Boost content. The curriculum isn't released yet, but Bench Boost is open to explore today.

Bench Boost is in beta

It is actively in development. Content and features are still growing, and your feedback helps. This website does not collect student data, and Bench Boost runs separately at learn.evolutionpe.com.

Good to know

Questions

Where do I find Bench Boost?

Bench Boost lives at learn.evolutionpe.com. This website links to it and does not host it.

What does it cost?

You can open Bench Boost today. Future pricing hasn't been decided, and nothing on this website takes payments.

Is it okay to use with my class?

That is up to your school or district. Please check their rules about students using online tools before you start.

Can I suggest a topic or report a mistake?

Yes, please. Use the feedback link and tell us what you found.
