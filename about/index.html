About

Built by a teacher who wanted better tools.

Evolution PE was created by an elementary Physical Education and Health teacher to make PE easier to run, more inclusive, and honest about what it can do.

Our mission

Better PE for every child and every teacher

Good physical education shouldn't depend on how much prep time a teacher has or how much equipment a family owns. We build curriculum, learning activities, and tools that are clear, flexible, and ready to use.

How we work

Four commitments

Movement is never punishment

We design games so children re-enter through a skill, not by doing exercise as a penalty.

Everyone gets a role

Every game has solo, partner, and group versions, so a child is never stuck waiting for a teammate.

Show the sources

Health ideas in our materials name where they come from, so you can check and read more.

Say what's ready

If something is in beta or coming soon, the page says so. We don't claim approvals we don't have.
